Part 2: up the mountain (concepts)

World 1 · Puff Ball Planet Part 2: you climb a mountain. The camera follows you up as well as across: switchback ledges, a rope bridge over a gorge, ladders, a checkpoint flower and nuggies on the way. Three possible looks for the same climb. The mountain goats from your drawing: they patrol the ledges and charge at you horns-first.

Marshmallow Mountain

The same candy world as Part 1, stood on end: lilac rock, puff-moss ledges dusted with snow, soda waterfalls, the ringed planet behind the peaks.

Frosty Puff Peaks

Higher and colder: icy blue rock, thick puff-snow on every ledge, frosted pines, snow drifting down. Ice ledges could be slippery.

Sunset Crags

Warm canyon cliffs at golden hour: orange rock, dry golden grass, wooden ladders and rope bridges, dust drifting in the light.

The climb, top to bottom

A first idea for the route: base camp, switchbacks, the rope-bridge gorge, goat cliffs, ladders and soda falls, icy ledges with gusts of wind, a falling-rock chute, then the summit gate. Like Part 1, each checkpoint is guarded by a Giant.
