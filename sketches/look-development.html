Puffy · look development 2

How should Puffy look?

Four ways to draw Puffy, the bad guys and their world, all hand-drawn but with different amounts of polish. Every option uses the same drawings from your sketches; only the line and color treatment changes.

Drawn from these sketches. Each strip shows Puffy (idle, running, jumping, puffed up when hit), a Puffball, and Big Puff calm and then angry after his first hit.

ACrayon storybook

Closest to the original drawings: graphite pencil lines and crayon-colored fills with the paper showing through.

Good
Feels like the drawings came to life
Watch out
Busier texture; small details can get noisy on a phone

BPolished hand-drawn In game now

Cleaner, rounder shapes with soft shading and a shine, but the lines still wobble and a fine crayon grain stays in the color.

Good
Reads well at small sizes and still feels hand-made
Watch out
Less of the original crayon charm

CInk & watercolor

Loose brush-ink lines that swell and break, with watercolor washes bleeding a little past the lines.

Good
Most artistic and distinctive
Watch out
Softer edges; enemies and platforms can be harder to spot

DBold sticker

Chunky outlines and bright flat color with one hard shadow and a shine.

Good
Most readable and punchy on a phone
Watch out
Least hand-drawn; closer to a typical mobile game
How to pick: tell me a letter, or mix them (for example "A's crayon color with B's shading"). The choice applies to everything: the three heroes, enemies, tiles, backgrounds and menus.
In the game right now: option B, drawn at 3× resolution so it stays sharp on phone screens.
