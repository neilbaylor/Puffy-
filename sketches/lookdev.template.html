How should Puffy look?

Four ways to draw Puffy and his world, all hand-drawn but with different amounts of polish. Every option uses the same drawing of Puffy from your sketches, only the line and color treatment changes, and the world beside him is drawn the same way.

Drawn from these two sketches: the orange egg with one big eye, flappy arms and long legs, and the round, spiky puffed-up form (shown in the last pose of each strip, used when he gets hit).

How to pick: tell me a letter, or mix them (for example "A's crayon color with B's shading"). The choice applies to everything: the three heroes, enemies, tiles, backgrounds and menus.
In the game right now: option B, drawn at 3× resolution so it stays sharp on phone screens.
