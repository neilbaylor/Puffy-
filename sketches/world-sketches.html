World sketches

Five directions for the game's worlds, drawn with the three placeholder heroes. Each sketch is one slice of a level, showing the ground style, backdrop, hazards and enemy types. Sunny Hills is already playable as level 1.

Sunny Hills, in the game now Playable

01Sunny Hills Level 1

Bright opening world. Green checker hills, palm trees, floating ? blocks.

Signature hazards
  • Brick walls you blast through
  • Pits crossed on wooden platforms
  • Cannon turrets on stone blocks
Enemies
Beetles, spiky beetles, hoppers, bugs

02Dune Canyon

Late-afternoon desert. Striped sandstone, rock arches, a huge low sun.

Signature hazards
  • Quicksand slows you and pulls you down
  • Tumbleweeds roll through
  • Gems hidden in sandstone blocks
Enemies
Scorpion shells, vultures, sand-burrowers

03Frosty Peaks

Snowy night under the northern lights. Ice bricks, pines, crystals.

Signature hazards
  • Slippery ice floors (harder to stop)
  • Icicles that drop when you pass
  • Rockets shatter frozen walls
Enemies
Snow blobs, ice-spike shells, penguin sliders

04Cloud Kingdom

Sunset sky with floating islands over a sea of clouds. A castle in the distance.

Signature hazards
  • Cloud platforms that crumble
  • Wind gusts push you mid-jump
  • Falling means a long drop
Enemies
Winged puffs, balloon bombers, birds

05Magma Works

Volcano factory at night. Girders on chains, hazard stripes, lava below.

Signature hazards
  • Conveyor belts
  • Lava that rises and falls
  • Swinging girder platforms
Enemies
Robot walkers, fire-spike shells, lava jumpers
How to pick: tell me which worlds you like (or mix them, e.g. "Frosty Peaks look with Cloud Kingdom platforms"). The game draws every tile, background and color from one theme table, so re-skinning a level is quick. These sketches use placeholder heroes until your character sketches arrive.
